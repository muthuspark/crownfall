# Crownfall Design

## Scene

A player at a laptop settles into an unhurried afternoon skirmish. Warm parchment controls frame a sunlit, green isometric countryside.

## Palette

Moss and wheat terrain; warm charcoal panels; ivory text; restrained ochre for actions and selection. Blue banners for the player and red pennants for the opponent. CSS tokens use OKLCH.

## Typography

Georgia for evocative titles; system sans-serif for all commands, labels, and data. Tabular resource numerals.

## Layout

Full-window battlefield, slim resource bar, contextual bottom command dock, bottom-right minimap, small top-left learning objectives. Responsive desktop layout at 1280×720 and above.

## Interaction

Crisp terrain and building art with disabled image smoothing. Villagers use original pre-rendered 3D sprites with natural proportions, eight facing directions, team-colored clothing, and tools appropriate to their work. Sprite cells are smoothly downsampled before being drawn into the battlefield. Selection rings, destination markers, construction previews, health bars, and short inline notices. Menus use semantic HTML. Reduced motion freezes villager poses and suppresses decorative movement.
