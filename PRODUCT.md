# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Desktop players seeking a compact, approachable medieval RTS skirmish using keyboard and mouse.

## Product Purpose

A complete 20–30 minute browser-local match: gather, construct, advance, train, fight, win or lose. One relaxed AI, one designed map, two ages, three resources, 60 population.

## Brand Personality

Tactile, pastoral, deliberate. Original retro isometric art inspired by the readability of classic strategy games.

## Anti-references

No dashboard-like card grids, neon fantasy, unrelated marketing screens, copied commercial artwork, or heavy cinematic UI obscuring gameplay.

## Design Principles

- The battlefield is the primary interface.
- Make commands and their consequences readable.
- Teach through small optional objectives.
- Keep simulation, saves, and assets entirely on the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable HTML controls, visible keyboard focus, descriptive labels, team symbols as well as colors, reduced-motion support, no audio-dependent information. The battlefield requires spatial pointing input; full nonvisual gameplay is outside this MVP.
